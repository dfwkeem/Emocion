Chiamaka, If My Heart Could Speak

If my heart could speak, it would start slow,
like someone tiptoeing into a quiet room.
It would say your name the way you do,
soft, and in no hurry.

It would talk about your smile first,
how it arrives like good news
and makes a heavy day feel light.
I still can’t explain the effect.
I only know it works.

Then the way you talk,
easy, warm, never rushed.
I come with plans for what to say
and leave with nothing but a grin.

It would tell you what it sees in your heart,
a kindness that never asks to be noticed.
I notice it anyway.
I hope that’s okay.

It would thank you for your calm,
the gentle way you carry yourself
when everything else is loud.
Near you, the world slows down enough to enjoy.

It would make no grand promises.
It can’t see tomorrow, only today,
and today says you are worth knowing better.

So here is what it wants, plain and small:
to keep learning who you are,
one talk at a time,
at the speed that feels right to you.

And if this friendship turns into something more,
I will be glad, and gentle with it.
If it doesn’t, I am still happy you’re here.
That is everything my heart would say.

Kim
